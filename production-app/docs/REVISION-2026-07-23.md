## Workflow corrections applied

- User-facing term is **Budget Request**, not Shipment Request.
- User-facing decision area is **Approval**.
- CEO/DCS payment area is **DCS for Payment**.
- Every role lands on Shipment Profitability; Requesters see only their own shipments.
- Mich is spelled **Mich** throughout.
- Mich prepares Billing, monitors Liquidation, verifies proof, and closes variance.
- The Requester records their own actual Liquidation expenses and receipts.
- Draft Billing and replacement proposals require GM or Administrator approval before finalization; finalized Billing is immutable.
- Administrator owns voids, funding sources, and tax profiles. GM owns all financial approvals, including Credit Memos.
- Bridge PH/Admin can review attributable security, transaction, workflow, configuration, and document activity without device surveillance.

## Boundaries still requiring production decisions

- Private Backblaze upload, signature/size validation, SHA-256 metadata, authorized indexing, and authenticated inline no-store streaming are connected. Document download controls are disabled. Production still requires approved malware scanning and retention.
- Professional A4 browser Print / Save PDF is available, but server-rendered archival PDF and formal BIR document approval remain future controls.
- The Accounting CSV is intentionally neutral. QuickBooks/Xero-specific import mapping requires the actual chart of accounts and accountant validation.
- The Philippine tax adviser must approve the active VAT/CWT profiles, formal document terminology, numbering, and required statutory fields before production.
